# Article SEO Best Practices

## Heading Structure for SEO

To get the best SEO scores, all articles should follow proper heading hierarchy:

### ✅ Correct Structure

Every article automatically gets an **H1** (the article title).

Within your article content, use headings like this:

```
[Article Title - automatic H1]

[Introduction paragraph]

## Main Section (H2)
[Content about this section]

### Subsection (H3)
[More detailed content]

### Another Subsection (H3)  
[More content]

## Another Main Section (H2)
[Content]
```

### ❌ Common Mistakes

1. **No headings at all** - Just paragraphs with no H2/H3 structure
2. **Skipping levels** - Going from H1 directly to H3 (skip H2)
3. **Using bold instead of headings** - Bold text doesn't count for SEO

## How to Add Headings in the Editor

When writing articles:

1. Use the **H2** button for main sections
2. Use the **H3** button for subsections
3. Don't use H1 (that's automatic - the article title)

## Internal Linking Strategy

**Link to related articles within your content!** This improves SEO and helps readers discover more content.

### Best Practices for Internal Links

1. **Link naturally** - Add links where they make sense in the context:
   ```
   As we discussed in our article on [Constitutional Rights](/articles/constitutional-rights),
   the First Amendment protects...
   ```

2. **Use descriptive anchor text** - Link text should describe what the reader will find:
   - ✅ Good: "Read our guide on [civic engagement strategies](/articles/civic-engagement)"
   - ❌ Bad: "Click [here](/articles/civic-engagement) to learn more"

3. **Link to 2-3 related articles** per article - Don't overdo it, but connect related content

4. **Link early and throughout** - Add links in introduction and within section content

### How to Add Links in the Editor

1. Highlight the text you want to link
2. Click the **🔗 Link** button in the toolbar  
3. Enter the URL to another article: `/articles/article-slug`
4. The link will open in the same window automatically

### Example with Internal Links

```markdown
The challenges facing American policy require comprehensive solutions.
As outlined in our [Economic Policy Framework](/articles/economic-policy-framework),
we must prioritize sustainable growth.

## Trade Agreements

International trade policy intersects with our domestic priorities.
See our analysis of [current trade negotiations](/articles/trade-policy-2024)
for detailed context on bilateral agreements.
```

## Content Depth & Citation-Ready Information

**Write with specifics!** Search engines favor content with concrete details, facts, and examples.

### What Makes Content "Citation-Ready"

- **Specific dates**: "In January 2024" not "recently"
- **Statistics and data**: "73% of voters" not "most voters"  
- **Named sources**: "According to the Congressional Budget Office" not "experts say"
- **Concrete examples**: Real cases, specific policies, actual legislation
- **Detailed explanations**: Why, how, and what specifically happened

### Before & After Examples

❌ **Vague**: "Many people support this policy because it's good for the economy."

✅ **Citation-Ready**: "According to a March 2024 Pew Research poll, 68% of Americans support expanded job training programs. The Congressional Budget Office estimates this policy would create 125,000 new jobs over five years while reducing the deficit by $2.1 billion through increased tax revenue."

❌ **Generic**: "The border crisis is getting worse and we need solutions."

✅ **Specific**: "U.S. Customs and Border Protection reported 2.4 million encounters at the southern border in fiscal year 2023, a 37% increase from 2022. Texas allocated $4.2 billion in state funds for border security operations in the 2024-2025 biennium budget."

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Article has a clear title (auto H1)
- [ ] Content is divided into 2-3 main sections (H2)
- [ ] Each main section has 2-3 subsections (H3)
- [ ] Headings describe the content below them
- [ ] Headings follow logical hierarchy (H2 → H3, never skip)
- [ ] **2-3 internal links to related articles** with descriptive anchor text
- [ ] **Specific facts, dates, and statistics** instead of vague claims
- [ ] **Named sources** when citing data or expert opinions
- [ ] **Concrete examples** that readers can verify

## Why This Matters

Search engines use multiple signals to rank content:

**Heading structure** helps them:
- Understand your content hierarchy  
- Create article snippets in search results
- Determine relevance to search queries
- Improve accessibility for screen readers

**Internal links** help them:
- Discover and index more of your content
- Understand topic relationships
- Keep readers on your site longer
- Build topic authority

**Specific, citation-ready content** helps them:
- Identify your article as authoritative
- Match precise search queries
- Trust your content for featured snippets
- Rank you higher for fact-based searches

Proper structure + internal links + detailed content = Better SEO = More readers finding your articles!
