# Article SEO Best Practices

## Heading Structure for SEO

To get the best SEO scores, all articles should follow proper heading hierarchy:

### ✅ Correct Structure

Every article automatically gets an **H1** (the article title).

Within your article content, use headings like this:

```
[Article Title - automatic H1]

[Introduction paragraph]

## Main Section (H2)
[Content about this section]

### Subsection (H3)
[More detailed content]

### Another Subsection (H3)  
[More content]

## Another Main Section (H2)
[Content]
```

### ❌ Common Mistakes

1. **No headings at all** - Just paragraphs with no H2/H3 structure
2. **Skipping levels** - Going from H1 directly to H3 (skip H2)
3. **Using bold instead of headings** - Bold text doesn't count for SEO

## How to Add Headings in the Editor

When writing articles:

1. Use the **H2** button for main sections
2. Use the **H3** button for subsections
3. Don't use H1 (that's automatic - the article title)

## Example Article Structure

**Title:** "The Future of American Policy"  
*(This becomes H1 automatically)*

**Content:**

```markdown
America faces challenges that require thoughtful solutions...

## Economic Policy
Our economic approach focuses on three key areas...

### Tax Reform
Current tax policies need adjustment because...

### Trade Agreements  
International trade must prioritize...

## Foreign Policy
Our stance on international relations...

### Alliance Building
Strong alliances require...

### National Security
Protecting our borders means...
```

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Article has a clear title (auto H1)
- [ ] Content is divided into 2-3 main sections (H2)
- [ ] Each main section has 2-3 subsections (H3)
- [ ] Headings describe the content below them
- [ ] Headings follow logical hierarchy (H2 → H3, never skip)

## Why This Matters

Search engines use heading structure to:
- Understand your content hierarchy  
- Create article snippets in search results
- Determine relevance to search queries
- Improve accessibility for screen readers

Proper headings = Better SEO = More readers finding your articles!
